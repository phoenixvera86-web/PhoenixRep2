### Multiplication

In mathematics, we use symbols to represent this repeated addition:

$$3 \times 4 = 12$$

* **3** is the number of groups or boxes (the *multiplier*).
* **$\times$** is the multiplication sign.
* **4** is the number of items inside each group (the *multiplicand*).
* **$=$** is the equal sign.
* **12** is the total answer (the *product*).
