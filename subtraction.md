# Subtraction of Integers

To subtract an integer, add its opposite:

`a - b = a + (-b)`

The opposite of a positive integer is negative, and the opposite of a negative integer is positive.

## Example

`5 - (-3) = 5 + 3 = 8`

Subtracting negative 3 is the same as adding positive 3. Start at 5 on a number line and move 3 places to the right to reach 8.
